### 💡 About the Organization
**RatelSlop** (casually referenced as *"RatelSlop Studios"*) is an informal, non-commercial collective moniker used by independent hobby contributors based in the Netherlands and Germany. We are **not** a company, legal entity, or formal studio.

Projects across this organization are maintained independently by their respective contributors and may be deployed on different platforms (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages/Workers) under various domains (including [schoolnaam.nl](https://schoolnaam.nl) and [hooijmaijers.me](https://hooijmaijers.me)).

### ⚖️ Intellectual Property & Licensing
We do not apply a blanket license across all repositories:

1. **Specified Licenses:** If a repository contains an explicit license file (such as MIT, Apache 2.0, or GPL), the terms of that license govern the use and distribution of its code.
2. **Default (All Rights Reserved):** If a repository does not include an explicit license file, standard copyright applies by default and all rights are reserved by the respective author(s).
3. **Branding & Hub Assets:** The RatelSlop name, logo, and the design/assets of [schoolnaam.nl](https://schoolnaam.nl) are proprietary and all rights are reserved.

### 🔒 Hosting, Infrastructure & Privacy
- **Independent Project Policies:** Each project follows its own privacy and technical practices. While the central hub ([schoolnaam.nl](https://schoolnaam.nl)) does not use commercial trackers, advertising networks, or monetize user data, we cannot make guarantees for external deployments or independent projects. Please consult the specific project repository or linked domain for its individual privacy practices.
- **Decentralized Deployments:** Projects use various infrastructure providers (such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare). Technical connection data (such as IP addresses) is processed directly by the hosting provider in question in accordance with their respective infrastructure policies.
- **Hub Policies:** For the central hub, see our [Privacy Policy](https://schoolnaam.nl/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://schoolnaam.nl/terms).
