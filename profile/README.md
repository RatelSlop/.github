### 💡 About the Organization
**RatelSlop** (casually referenced as *"RatelSlop Studios"*) is an informal, non-commercial collective moniker used by independent hobby contributors based in the Netherlands and Germany. We are **not** a company, legal entity, or formal studio.

Projects across this organization are developed independently by our contributors and may be deployed on different platforms (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages/Workers) under various domains (including [schoolnaam.nl](https://schoolnaam.nl) and [hooijmaijers.me](https://hooijmaijers.me)).

---

### ⚖️ Intellectual Property & Licensing
We do not apply a blanket license across all repositories. **Each repository is independent**:

1. **Per-Repository Terms:** Each project contains its own `LICENSE` file determining whether it is open source (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL) or proprietary.
2. **Default (All Rights Reserved):** Unless a repository explicitly provides an open-source license, all rights are reserved by the respective contributor(s) who authored the code.
3. **Branding & Hub Assets:** The RatelSlop branding, logo, and the design of [schoolnaam.nl](https://schoolnaam.nl) are proprietary and all rights are reserved.

---

### 🔒 Hosting, Infrastructure & Privacy
- **Decentralized Deployments:** Different projects use different infrastructure providers (e.g. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Workers/Pages). Technical connection logging (such as IP addresses) is processed at the infrastructure level by the respective provider.
- **Privacy First:** We do not track users, run advertising networks, or monetize personal data across any of our tools.
- **Policies:** For the central hub, see our [Privacy Policy](https://schoolnaam.nl/privacy) and [Terms of Service](https://schoolnaam.nl/terms). Individual third-party platforms and linked domains operate under their respective infrastructure policies.
